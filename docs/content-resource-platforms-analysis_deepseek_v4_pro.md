## 5. Strategic Recommendations for IB Learning Site Project

### 5.1 Content Structure Reference (How to Model Your Content)

|-------------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Revision Notes** | Save My Exams | Examiner-written, syllabus-organized, with diagrams and exam tips. Concise but comprehensive. |

### 5.2 Content Quality Bar

- **Revision Village's 85% market penetration** means IB student expectations are shaped by RV's quality standard. Your content must match or exceed:
  - Video solutions for complex problems
  - Topic-by-topic question organization with difficulty scaling
  - AI-powered answer feedback
  - Mobile-accessible flashcards

- **Save My Exams' examiner credentials** set the standard for notes: written by actual examiners, with explicit syllabus mapping, exam tips, and common mistake callouts.

### 5.3 Feature Architecture Reference

| Feature | Reference Implementation | Notes for Your Project |

| Content Delivery | Questionbank + Video (RV) / Notes + Practice (SME) | Mix of static content (notes) + interactive (questions) + multimedia (video solutions). |
